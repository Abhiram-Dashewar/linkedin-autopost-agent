📄Overview

This application is a personal automation tool used to create and publish LinkedIn content through the LinkedIn API.

🔍Information We Access

The application may access LinkedIn account information and permissions necessary to publish posts on behalf of the authorized user.

⚙️How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the LinkedIn API is used only to provide the application's content creation and publishing functionality.

🤝Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

🗄️Data Retention

The application retains only the information necessary for its functionality and operation.

✉️Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the application owner through the GitHub profile associated with this project.
